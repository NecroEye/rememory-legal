Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

No account, local-first

ReMemory AI keeps your study material on your phone. There is no sign-up and no account. The questions you photograph, the notes, drawings and voice recordings you add, your review history and your statistics are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive them, and we have no way to reach them.

There is one exception, and Section 5 explains it in full: the optional AI features send question text, and on request a question photo, to Google's Gemini service so it can suggest a subject or explain the question.

  • Your name, email address or phone number; there is no account to put them in

  • Browsing history or activity in other apps

4. On-Device Processing

Runs on your phone
  • Text recognition reads the words in your question photos using Google ML Kit, with the model built into the app. It works without a connection and sends nothing.
  • Camera is used only while you photograph a question. Photos are saved inside the app, not to your gallery.
  • Microphone is used only while you record a voice note for your own solution. The recording is saved inside the app.
  • Reminders are scheduled on the device. No server sends them.
  • Read aloud uses your device's text-to-speech engine. Some voices offered by that engine need a connection, and those are processed by the engine's provider under its own terms; the app marks them.

5. AI Features (Google Gemini)

When AI features are available in your region and version of the app, ReMemory uses Google's Gemini API through Firebase AI Logic. Two different things can be sent, and they are sent at different times.

Sent automatically
  • After you save a question, if its text was recognised clearly and your phone is online, the recognised text is sent once to suggest a subject, a topic and a few tags.
  • The photo itself is not sent for this. If the text could not be read reliably, nothing is sent.
Sent only when you ask
  • When you tap Explain or ask for practice questions, the question photo and its recognised text are sent so the model can see the question.

These requests carry no account, name or email. The answers are saved on your device next to the question, labelled as AI content, and the app asks you to check them against your own photo.

Google processes these requests as a data processor under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service. Under those terms, for our paid use of the API, Google does not use your prompts, images or the responses to improve its products, and keeps them only for a limited period to detect abuse and meet legal obligations.

Avoid photographing anything you would not want processed by an online service, such as a page carrying your full name, student number or other personal details. You can crop a page before saving it.

ReMemory uses the following services, each with its own privacy policy:

7. No Advertising

ReMemory shows no ads and contains no advertising network. The Advertising ID mentioned in Section 2 is read only by Google Analytics for Firebase to count usage. It is a resettable identifier; you can reset it or delete it in your device settings under Google > Ads.

8. Purchases & Subscriptions

ReMemory offers an optional subscription, ReMemory Pro. All payments are processed by Google Play Billing; we never see or store your payment card details.

To activate and restore your subscription, purchase receipts are validated by RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and Google Play purchase tokens. No name, email or payment details are shared with RevenueCat.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can cancel at any time in Google Play > Subscriptions. Refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policy.

9. Sharing, Exporting & Backups

Only when you choose

ReMemory never uploads your collection. You can move copies out of the app yourself:

  • Backup and export writes a ZIP backup, a CSV or JSON file, or a printable PDF to a location you pick in the system file picker. If you pick a cloud folder there, that provider's terms apply to the file.
  • Share a deck creates a file with the questions, photos and your solutions in that deck, and hands it to the app you choose in the share sheet. Anyone you send it to can read it.

10. Storage, Retention & Deletion

Your questions, photos, notes and history are stored locally on your device, inside the app's private storage. Data we receive from the services in Section 6 is sent over encrypted connections.

  • Delete all data in Settings removes every question, photo and review record from the device.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its local data. Files you exported or shared stay where you put them.
  • Crash reports and analytics are kept by Google for the retention periods set in Firebase, after which they are deleted.

11. Your Choices

  • Camera, microphone and notifications: each is requested only when first needed, and you can revoke it in Android settings
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in device settings under Google > Ads
  • AI: photos are only sent when you tap Explain or ask for practice questions; subject suggestions use recognised text only
  • Your collection: export it, or delete it from Settings, at any time

12. Children's Privacy

ReMemory is a study app for exam students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under the age of majority where you live, use the app with the involvement of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will help remove it.

13. International Data Transfers

Crash, analytics and AI requests may be processed on servers outside your country of residence, operated by Google and RevenueCat. These transfers are carried out under those providers' safeguards and applicable data protection law.

14. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR and Turkey's KVKK, you may have the right to:

  • Access the data held about you
  • Have it corrected or deleted
  • Object to or restrict its processing
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

Because your study material never leaves your device except as described above, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app. For anything else, contact us.
